# Ambiguities and literal choices

1. **13.3[C], “reading-order tokens are exactly a member then 700.”** Readings considered: retain `000` and line-break structure versus use the analysis layer’s reading-order legible-sign sequence. Choice: use the latter. Section 13’s general rule says only legible signs are signs, and the named procedure treats this two-sign family as a sign sequence. Affects `13.3.*.C_slot.family_member_then_700` and `family_pct_of_multisign`.
2. **13.7[07-C], “stems (the reading-order tokens without the final one).”** Choice: stems are the legible reading-order signs before the eligible final sign, excluding `000` and `999`. This follows the section-13 data-layer rule and the named procedure’s gated sequences. Affects `13.7.*.C_terminals`.
3. **13.8 appendix G4b, mean length by tier.** Choice: report mean legible length over all multi-sign SEAL:S texts in each tier, not only end-marker-eligible texts. The adjacent rate quantities use section-5 eligibility; the named G4 procedure’s length description is over the multi-sign seal population. Affects `13.8.*.appendix_G4.G4b_mean_length`.
4. **13.17 and 13.18[F6], perturbation identity.** Choice: transform only legible signs inside the full section-2 text key, preserving end marks, line structure, and `000` placeholders. This is the literal consequence of “keys” and “perturbations change legible signs only.” Affects `13.17.*` and `13.18.*.F6`.
